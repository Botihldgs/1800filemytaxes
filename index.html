Questions people ask us

Is the tax readiness quiz really free?

Yes. The quiz and your personal checklist are free, with no obligation. You only pay if you book a consultation.

What does the one on one consultation cost?

The consultation is $59. If you choose to work with us on your tax prep, the full $59 is credited toward your fee. You see the price before you book.

Do you work with self-employed people and 1099 earners?

Yes, that is who we help most. Freelancers, gig workers, side hustlers and small business owners often have the most deductions to find and the most to gain from planning ahead.

Do I have to wait for tax season?

No. We help year round, including prior year returns, amended returns and planning questions. The sooner you start, the more options you have.

How do I check on my refund?

The IRS runs the official refund tracker. Your status is usually available about 24 hours after you e-file a current year return and about 4 weeks after you mail a paper return. Use the IRS Where's My Refund page, and see the IRS tools section above for amended returns.

Do I need to make quarterly estimated tax payments?

If you are self-employed and expect to owe $1,000 or more when you file, you generally do. Payments are due in April, June, September and January. Use the quarterly payment calculator above for a starting number, then book a consultation to confirm it.

How much should a 1099 worker set aside for taxes?

Many freelancers set aside 25% to 30% of what they net, but it depends on income, filing status and deductions. The self-employment tax calculator above shows your own number. Self-employment tax alone is 15.3% on 92.35% of your net profit, up to the Social Security wage base.

What documents do I need to file?

It depends on your situation. Take the quiz and you get a checklist built around your answers. In general you need ID, Social Security numbers, income forms such as W-2s and 1099s, and records of your expenses.

What if I have not filed in years?

File as soon as you can. The late filing penalty is 5% of the unpaid tax per month, up to 25%, and it is much larger than the late payment penalty. If you are owed a refund there is usually no penalty. We help with prior year and unfiled returns.

What if I owe and cannot pay?

You still want to file. Filing on time avoids the larger penalty, and the IRS offers payment plans. Our sister company 1800TaxSupport helps with larger IRS balances.

What if I got a letter from the IRS?

Do not ignore it. Bring it to your consultation and we will explain what it means and what to do next. Replying on time keeps your options open.

How accurate are the calculators?

They are estimates built on 2026 federal rules: the 2026 standard deduction, tax brackets and Social Security wage base. They do not include state tax, additional Medicare tax, most credits or special situations. A consultation turns the estimate into a real plan.

Do you serve people outside the Carolinas?

Yes. We serve taxpayers nationwide.

Is my information safe?

We treat your information as confidential and never sell it.
